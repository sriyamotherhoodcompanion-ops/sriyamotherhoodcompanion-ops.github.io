How to Upgrade

  1. Open the SRIYA app and go to the Plans screen.

  2. Choose Paid or Premium, and Monthly, Annual or Lifetime.

  3. Pay securely with Google Play. Your plan unlocks straight away.

  4. Manage or cancel a subscription any time in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

Important: SRIYA is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Always talk to your doctor or midwife about your health and your pregnancy.
